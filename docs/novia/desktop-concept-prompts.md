# PC 端设计图生成提示词

使用内置 ImageGen，以[移动端参考图](mobile-reference.jpg)作为视觉输入。第二轮以初稿为输入，修正用户消息的归属。最终采用[修订后的设计图](desktop-concept.png)。

## 初稿

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Adapt the attached mobile mockup into a NEW, separate widescreen desktop UI design for Novia (诺薇娅), a personal AI assistant for chat and delegating work. Image 1 is the visual source: keep the fantasy heroine's recognizable silver-lavender hair, face, and dusk wisteria-and-castle ambiance, the deep plum translucent glass, pale lavender edges, warm candle glow, rounded cards, and centered immersive feel. Translate the three mobile views into one desktop app window. Use a 16:10 or 16:9 landscape canvas, a front-on, full-bleed crisp product screenshot with no device bezel, no external labels and no decorative title. Render one state: left collapsible sidebar open, central chat, right collapsible task details open. The right settings gear stays in the top-right of the app and would replace the right task panel when clicked. Left sidebar width about 17%, center 61%, right side 22%. Keep the chat as the main focus. Make the scenic image span the whole viewport. Place the heroine's face and upper body in the upper center-right within the central area, leaving the lower central area calm for chat bubbles and a fixed composer; do not hide her face behind a panel. The side panels use a darker, readable frosted plum surface. Keep generous spacing, deep plum shadows, pale ivory text, and subtle borders. Use realistic desktop app UI scale and clean readable Simplified Chinese type. Left sidebar text: “诺薇娅”, “业务与状态”, “对话记录”, “交办记录”, “定时任务”. Center text, exactly these short sample messages: user “整理这周的竞品动态。”, assistant “已交办，完成后我会告诉你。”, then a polished selected in-chat result card titled “竞品动态周报”, with a small green “已完成” status and “查看结果”. Composer placeholder “给诺薇娅发消息”. Right details text: “交办详情”, “竞品动态周报”, “已完成”, “执行者”, “Codex”, “结果”, “打开报告”. Include only icons for toggling the panels, settings, attach, and send. Strong text/background contrast and broad breathing room, no stats grids, no floating overlay labels, no second mockup, no markdown, no phone frame, no marketing copy or watermarks.
```

## 修订

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit, refinement of a high-fidelity Novia desktop UI concept. Image 1 is the exact edit target. Make one change only: in the central chat, the first message “整理这周的竞品动态。” is the USER's message. Remove the silver-haired Novia avatar beside that first message, and move just that first bubble horizontally to the right side of the central chat column, still at the same vertical position, aligned with the right edge of the bottom composer. It is an outgoing message with a slightly brighter soft lavender fill, no avatar, and the same readable white Chinese text. Keep the second message “已交办，完成后我会告诉你。” and its left-side character avatar exactly as before. Preserve the same 16:9 desktop framing and scale, character face and pose, full-screen fantasy wisteria backdrop, left business navigation, right task details panel and all panel labels, result card, settings gear, composer and icons. Do not add UI elements, text, logos, watermark, or other changes.
```
